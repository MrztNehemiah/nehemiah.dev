04 — Anomaly Detection Engine DevSecOps

Anomaly Detector

Real-time HTTP traffic anomaly detector for cloud.ng, a Nextcloud-powered cloud storage platform. Watches all incoming traffic, learns what normal looks like from a rolling 30-minute baseline, and auto-responds — blocking aggressive IPs via iptables and alerting via Slack — within 10 seconds of detection.

DETECTION ENGINE

→Dual deque sliding windows — per-IP and global — over 60s
→Rolling 30-min baseline, recalculated every 60s
→z-score > 3.0 or rate > 5× baseline triggers anomaly flag
→4xx/5xx surge tightens per-IP thresholds automatically

RESPONSE & OBSERVABILITY

→iptables DROP + Slack alert within 10s of detection
→Backoff auto-unban — 10 min, 30 min, 2 hr, then permanent
→Live dashboard — banned IPs, req/s, top-10 sources, CPU/mem
→Structured audit log for every ban, unban, and recalculation
